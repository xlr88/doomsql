Account & Data Deletion for DoomSQL

How to delete your DoomSQL account and associated data

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and GDPR/CCPA regulations, DoomSQL provides complete control over your account and personal data. You can delete your account and all associated cloud-synced data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the DoomSQL app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Under the Account & Cloud Sync card at the top, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your deletion. Your account, profile information, and all cloud-stored question solve milestones will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request manual deletion of your account and cloud data by emailing our support team:

  • Email: omkarmaduguri000@proton.me
  • Subject: DoomSQL Account Deletion Request
  • Information Required: The Google Account email address you used to sign into DoomSQL.

Upon verification, all cloud-synced progress records, profile data, and Firebase authentication credentials linked to your email address will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your Google User ID, email, and display name.
  • Your cloud-synced question solve history and practice streaks.
  • Saved custom SQL query drafts.
